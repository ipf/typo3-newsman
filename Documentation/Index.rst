############
Newsman
############

A newsletter subscription form for `TYPO3`_ that subscribes visitors to a
`GNU Mailman 3`_ mailing list.

:Extension key: ``newsman``
:Package: ``ipf/newsman``
:Namespace: ``Ipf\NewsMan\``
:TYPO3 version: 13.4, 14.x
:PHP version: 8.1 or higher

.. _TYPO3: https://typo3.org
.. _GNU Mailman 3: https://www.mailman.info/

The extension ships its own content element, FlexForm, Fluid template and
stylesheet. It stores nothing in the TYPO3 database — Mailman stays the single
source of truth for the subscribers.
